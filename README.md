🎯 Darts App

▶ Launch Darts App

https://bezoon5.github.io/HopkinsDarts/

Tap the link to open the app in your browser. It works on iPad, iPhone, Android and computers. You don't need to install anything.

Add it to your Home Screen

iPad / iPhone (Safari)

	1.	Open the link above in Safari.
	2.	Tap Share (the square with an arrow), then Add to Home Screen.
	3.	Tap Add. Darts App opens full screen like a normal app.

Android (Chrome)

	1.	Open the link in Chrome.
	2.	Tap the ⋮ menu, then Add to Home screen (or Install app).

What it does

	•	Games: X01 (501, 301 or custom; double out; legs or sets), Killer (2–8 players), Cricket, Around the Clock, Shanghai, and Knockout nights (3–16 players, X01 or Cricket matches, draw on the TV). Stats for X01, practice, Cricket, Shanghai, Around the Clock and Killer.
	•	Handicap start scores, a bull-off for the throw, head-to-head records, and optional voice scoring (tap the microphone and say "treble twenty", "sixty", "next").
	•	TV scoreboard: on the TV's web browser (or a laptop or tablet) open https://bezoon5.github.io/HopkinsDarts/tv.html (a light page made for TV browsers), then scan its QR code with the phone camera, or tap the TV button (top left of the home screen) and type its 6-digit code. The TV follows the game live and remembers the code. Turn an iPad or iPhone sideways for a scoreboard-and-keypad layout that works well with AirPlay mirroring.
	•	Practice mode with targets.
	•	Stats: PPR, first 9, checkout %, 60+/100+/140+/180 and heat maps.
	•	Checkout guide for 170 and under, plus a full checkout chart.
	•	Scoring: tap the keypad, tap the dartboard, or use a camera.
	•	Caller voice with a choice of voices and a volume control.
	•	Four colour schemes: switch under Colours in Settings (the gear).
	•	Blues: royal blue #003798 / #0000FF and white
	•	Villans: claret #670E36, sky blue #95BFE5, yellow #FEE505 and white
	•	Chalk: chalk on a blackboard (board green, chalk white and chalk yellow, sketchy chalk lettering)
	•	Neon: neon yellow #E6FF00 / #FFF000, electric purple #6A0DAD / #551A8B and deep black #111111

Works offline: once opened with internet, the app keeps a copy and plays without Wi-Fi.

Stats are saved in the browser on each device. Link nicknames (e.g. Dad, Daddy, Dr Mark as Mark) in Settings so all their games count together. Back up stats in Settings (the gear tab, which also has the colours, caller, TV link and scoring options) (a gentle reminder appears about once a month). They don't sync between devices, and clearing Safari's website data deletes them.
