Round 1 aim for 1s, round 2 for 2s, and so on. Only that number scores (double ×2, treble ×3). Hit its single, double and treble in one visit — a Shanghai — and you win outright. Most points after the last round wins.

Stats backup

Stats are kept on this device only.

Save a backup to Files now and then, or to move your stats to another iPad or laptop. Restoring adds what's missing and never doubles anything up.
